# Guia do Campo de Treinamento - A step-by-step Tutorial for Tutorial Island

Because some new players gets confused on certain things in the Tutorial and probably miss some things. 

Doing these steps will be extremely helpful on your journey.

\- 1. Talk to Boatman and select the 1st option for free Training Coins

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-1.png)

\- 2. Talk to the Castle Hostess and select the 1st option for free Training Coins. Talk to him again and select the 2nd option to get the more Training Coins

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-2.png)

\- 3. Open your inventory and buy Ordinary Knife and Recall Badge. Equip the Knife after purchase

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-3.png)

\- 4. Talk to the Ashamed Novice and he will complain about the two Novice Twins bullying him. Select the 1st option and he will ask you to talk to his cousin Johab

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-4.png)

\- 5. Follow the directions and talk to Johab. You will get Training Coins as a reward

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-5.png)

\- 6. Follow the directions and talk to the Twins. A cutscene will play. You will get Training Coins as a reward

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-6.png)

\- 7. Follow the directions and talk to the Ashamed Novice. You will get Training Coins as a reward. Move a couple of cells and he will then ask you to talk to Amora

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-7.png)

\- 8. Follow the directions and talk to Amora. She will ask you to kill Porings and get at least 5 Fruit Baskets which will be dropped into your inventory if you killed Porings. The drop rate isnt 100%. Just keep killing Porings until you get at least 5 Fruit Baskets

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-8.png)

* Before we start killing Porings, open your Skill Tree and place a skill point in Pick Up. Always max this one first before doing anything.

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-9.png)

* Assign some stat points. Dont worry about the stats. It will be reset after Job Change. Assign the stats however you see fit.

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-10.png)

* Buy the Training Shield, Training Uniform and Potion.

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-11.png)

\- 9. After killing Porings and gathering the Fruit Baskets. Return back to Amora and you will get Training Coins as reward. You will get additional rewards if give more than 5 Fruit Baskets

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-12.png)

10. Amora will ask you to talk to the Leonard the Monster Researcher. Select the 1st option for free Training Coins. Talk to him again to officially start the tutorial for the Hunting Quest. Select the Main Quest

![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-13.png)
![Campo de treinamento](/treinamento/treinamento-14.png)

>After this, you are basically free to do whatever you want but I highly suggest talking to the other NPCs around the castle before doing anything else
